# Che tempo fa, Nonna?

Nonna video-call weather speaking game for Arianna.

## How to play

Open https://appuccinohub.github.io/che-tempo-fa-nonna/ on a phone. Nonna is calling from Monte di Procida. Her Italian lines are recorded audio. Speaking needs a normal https URL. The tap fallback always works.

The microphone only works on a normal https URL (this Pages site, or `localhost`). A plain `http://` address, or a page opened as a file, will not get the mic.
